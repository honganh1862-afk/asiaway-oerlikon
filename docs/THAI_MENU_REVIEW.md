# Thai menu — review sheet

`data/thai_menu_EN_DE_VI.csv` is the Thai menu PDF (27.08.2024) as the app
serves it in **Winterthur only**, on its own *Thai* tab beside the Vietnamese
menu. Dish numbers, prices, options and spice levels are as printed. The German
is the printed German; the English descriptions and all Vietnamese text are
translations, and need checking by someone fluent before guests rely on them.

- **Allergens.** The Thai menu declares none ("ask our staff"), so every Thai

## Adapted from the print — please confirm

- **Each protein (and each fish sauce) is its own numbered dish**, e.g. 240.1
  Green Curry – Tofu … 240.6 – Schwein, as decided for the Vietnamese menu, so
  the kitchen always knows what was ordered. So "und Fleisch nach Wahl" became
  that dish's protein ("… geröstete Erdnüsse und Krevetten"), and each fish
  dish names its own sauce instead of "Sauce nach Wahl".
- **260, 262 and 263 describe one protein but offer several.** 260 says
  "Entenbrust", 262 "Rindshuft (CH)" and 263 "Meeresfrüchte". For the other
  options the description names that option's protein instead; the dish names
  follow the menu's own photo captions ("Fried Chicken with Fresh Ginger").
  263 offers no seafood option, though its printed name says seafood.
- **The Thai name** (e.g. *Kang Kiew Wan*) opens each description, and **the
  chili icons** close it: one = mildly spicy, two = spicy, three = very spicy.
- **Small fixes to the printed German:** "Bailikum" → Basilikum,
  "s charfe" → scharfe, and a few case endings (e.g. "mit scharf-saurem
  Dressing").
